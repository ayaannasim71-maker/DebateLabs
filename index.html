FAQ

Questions, answered.

Some basics before you join.

Do I need previous debate experience?

No. Beginners are welcome.

Do I have to attend every debate?

No. Participate when your schedule allows.

Is DebateLabs international?

The community is designed to welcome students from different countries and use flexible timing for live sessions.

Can I help without being a debater?

Yes. Research, fact-checking, moderation and community participation are all useful roles.

Where do I apply?

Click the glowing Join DebateLabs button below. It opens the official Google Form.
